Brand guidelines · Version 1.0 · October 2026
01 · Brand story

Leave richer
than you arrived.

The Wealth Table is a purposefully designed gathering where women connect around real opportunities, shared ambitions, and the future of wealth and ownership. It is where women bring more than their stories to the table: the businesses they are building, the investments they are exploring, the opportunities they are pursuing, and the connections they need to make their next move.

Founded by Udo Okonjo, Founder of Radiant Collective Capital. Held in Nairobi, Accra and Lagos, and expanding across Africa and beyond.

ConnectionMeaningful relationships over networking.
OpportunityReal introductions, investments, partnerships.
CollaborationParticipation and contribution, not pitches.
OwnershipFrom success to ownership.

03 · Clear space and size

Give it room.

X
X = half the height of the monogram

Keep a clear space of X (half the height of the WT monogram) on every side. No text, image edges or other logos inside it.

StackedMinimum 120 px wide · 30 mm
HorizontalMinimum 180 px wide · 45 mm
WordmarkMinimum 110 px wide · 28 mm
MonogramMinimum 32 px wide · 8 mm
SealMinimum 90 px · 22 mm

06 · Typography

Four typefaces, one voice.

Lexend Zetta · display capitals
Come to the table

Light (200/300), uppercase, wide. Headlines, labels and buttons on the website.

Bodoni Moda · editorial
Leave richer than you arrived.

Regular with italic emphasis. Posters, social headlines, quotes. Echoes the logo.

Hanken Grotesk · text
A purposefully designed gathering where women connect around real opportunities.

Light (300) and Regular (400). Paragraphs, captions, interface text.

Antonio · numerals

Condensed Light. Dates, fees, big numbers and the footer wordmark.

All four are open-source (Google Fonts, SIL OFL) and included in fonts/. Headlines: sentence case in Bodoni, UPPERCASE in Lexend. Keep one italic word per headline.

08 · Art and patterns

The table, seen from above.

The brand device is a round table: concentric ripples of conversation and seats around a ring. Use ripples as quiet backgrounds in gold or tonal hairlines; use seats as a pattern or avatar. Keep opacity low behind text.

09 · Photography

Warm, candid, documentary.

LookNatural light, real moments, white florals, candles.
PeopleLaughing, listening, speaking. Never posed stock.
CropFaces clear of text; keep the table in frame.
Quality1,500 px or wider, sharp. Do not use low-resolution or upscaled images.
CreditCredit the photographer and confirm usage rights.
TreatmentBlack and white for the founder; full colour elsewhere. No filters.
