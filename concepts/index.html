Concept round 2 · 28 Oct 2026

Four different ways to set the table.

Redone from scratch. Each concept now takes its look, structure, type, colour and motion from specific reference sites, so they no longer resemble one another. All four use light palettes, only the sharp event photographs, and the same real content. “Register your interest” is in the navigation, the hero, a sticky control and the closing section of every one, and opens a registration form wherever you click it. Open each on desktop and on your phone.

Concept 1

Warm Greige

Reads like Mejuma Tuscany: a lowercase serif wordmark spanning the whole screen, property-style cards for each gathering, stacking step cards and one yellow accent.
