 Nairobi - Where It All 
 Began. 
 DATE: 13 June 2026
 The inaugural edition of The Wealth Table brought together 24 remarkable women in Nairobi for an intimate, invitation-only gathering centred on wealth, ownership, opportunity and intentional connection.
In a room defined by openness, generosity and bold conversations, women shared experiences, challenged assumptions and explored what it truly means to build and own wealth on their own terms.
The word of the day was Courage a fitting beginning to a growing global conversation about women, wealth and ownership.

 ABOUT THE FOUNDER
 Udo
 Okonjo 
 Udo Okonjo believes the next chapter of women’s success must be about more than achievement. It must be about ownership.An entrepreneur, investor, board director, and founder of Radiant Collective Capital, Udo has spent decades championing women’s leadership, economic advancement, and the power of building wealth with intention.She created The Wealth Table from a simple conviction: when accomplished women come together for honest conversations about money, power, opportunity and ownership, extraordinary possibilities emerge.More than another networking event, The Wealth Table is a space for meaningful connections, shared intelligence, bold thinking, and the conversations that inspire women to make bigger moves.Her invitation is simple: Come to the table. Leave richer than you arrived.

 Frequently asked questions
 The Wealth Table FAQ
 1.⁠ ⁠What is The Wealth Table? 
 The Wealth Table is a purposefully designed gathering where women connect around real opportunities, shared ambitions, and the future of wealth and ownership.
It’s a place to bring what you’re building, explore what’s next, and connect with women whose knowledge, experience, relationships, and resources can open new possibilities.
From business opportunities and investments to strategic partnerships and valuable introductions, The Wealth Table creates the environment for connections to become meaningful action.
 2. Who is The Wealth Table for? 
 The Wealth Table is for women who are building, investing, leading, creating and ready to make their next significant move.
Whether you’re an entrepreneur exploring expansion, a professional building wealth, an investor seeking new perspectives or a leader looking to turn influence into ownership, there is a place for you at the table.
 3. Do I need to be a member of Radiant Collective Capital to attend? 
 No. The Wealth Table welcomes both RCC members and non-members. RCC members enjoy preferential participation rates.
 4. What can I expect at a gathering? 
 Expect an immersive experience built around meaningful connections, practical insights and real opportunities.
Come prepared to share what you’re working on, the opportunities you’re exploring, the challenges you’re navigating and the kind of connections that could help you move forward.
You may discover a potential collaborator, gain valuable investment intelligence, make an important introduction or uncover an opportunity you hadn’t previously considered.
Rather than conventional conference presentations or sales pitches, the focus is on participation, contribution and making things happen.
 5. Is there a participation fee? 
 Yes. Participation fees vary by city and gathering.
For The Wealth Table Lagos, participation is $200 for RCC members and $300 for non-members.
Both rates provide access to the same premium gathering and experience.
 6. How do I register for The Wealth Table Lagos? 
 Click “Register for Lagos,” complete the registration form, select your applicable participation rate, and proceed to payment.
Your registration will be confirmed following successful payment, subject to availability.
 7. When and where is The Wealth Table Lagos? 
 Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Civic Centre, Victoria Island, Lagos, from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM (WAT).
 8. Will The Wealth Table take place in other cities? 
 Yes. Following our inaugural gathering in Nairobi and our second gathering in Accra, The Wealth Table continues to expand across Africa and beyond, creating new spaces for women to connect, collaborate and unlock opportunities.
 9. How can I pay for my participation? 
 Participation fees are listed in US dollars, with the option to pay in Nigerian naira at the applicable local rate.
Secure online payment options and the applicable amounts will be displayed during registration.
Your participation will be confirmed once payment has been successfully completed.
